# #37 · Playwright E2E smoke flows

- **Status:** In review (PR #70)
- **Phase:** 7 - Hardening (pulled forward)

## Task

Three flows in a real browser at phone width: sign-in and role split, recording a
visit, an outing's lifecycle.

## Decisions

- **D-37-1 · The suite runs the standalone production server**, not `next start`.
  _Research:_ R-12. The server's settings were an env block; D-75-13 replaces it with a
  generated config file plus env secrets.
- ~~**D-37-2 · Pixel 5 on Chromium; Safari is uncovered.**~~ Superseded by D-32-18.
  _Research:_ R-14.
- **D-37-3 · The bottom nav does not render on public routes, and sign-in refreshes the
  router before navigating.** _Why:_ cached redirects broke sign-in in production.
  _Research:_ R-13.
- **D-37-4 · One worker, no retries, its own `door_to_door_e2e` database truncated per
  run.** _Why:_ D-07-4 makes parallel specs fight; a retry hides flakiness.
- **D-37-5 · Its own CI job.** Report uploaded on failure.
- **D-37-6 · Keep the suite thin** — layout, stacking, navigation. Vitest owns coverage.
- **D-37-7 · Setup goes through the API, not by driving screens; specs are idempotent,
  not order-dependent.**

## Open questions

- **PR #93 conflicts with D-37-3 (owner to decide).** D-37-3 says "sign-in refreshes the
  router before navigating"; commit bcd2360 replaced `router.refresh()` + `router.push()`
  with a full page load (`goTo` in `src/lib/navigate.ts`), because the refresh of
  `/sign-in` could outlive the push and, when it failed, hard-load `/sign-in` over the
  signed-in home. Not recorded as a decision until the owner agrees to supersede D-37-3.
